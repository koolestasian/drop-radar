# Career-product capability research and bigger-vision gaps

## What is already category capability, and what does T20 actually promise?

### Takeaway

Resume tailoring, application autofill, contact management, job timelines and interview preparation are established advertised product capabilities. T20 already anticipates most obvious ambitious extensions; the fair criticism is that much of this is planned, and several ideas lack a complete product contract, not that the vision contains none of them.

### Inferences

- "Build an AI career assistant" is too weak a distinguishing product definition: several products already advertise overlapping bundles. A stronger ambition is a continuous private system that knows the owner, tracks the changing recruiting world, acts within policy, and improves readiness over time.
- A founder-quality critique should separate three columns everywhere: shipped today, promised by T20, and proposed beyond/deeper than T20. Mixing them makes both ambition and criticism dishonest.
- Vendor feature marketing supports parity comparisons; it does not support comparative interview success or the claim that any vendor lacks Drop Radar's proposed integration.

### Gaps

- No independent hiring-ROI estimates or comparative submission reliability were established.
- LinkedIn official Top Applicant help was found in search but could not be retrieved. Do not build detailed LinkedIn capability comparisons from that snippet.
- JobCopilot contact page hit Firecrawl rate limits in this bounded pass; the preexisting report remains its documented comparison, not fresh verified retrieval.
- Clay-to-Mesh redirect observed now; the date/cause of rebrand was not researched. Say "the former Clay URL now redirects to Mesh" rather than guessing historical timing.

## Which seven larger product gaps should an ambitious founder attack?

### Takeaway

The largest expansion is from discovering/applying to jobs toward managing an entire competitive campaign: intelligence, access, proof, execution, growth and decisions. Several of these are already seeds inside T20 and should be described as making the vision concrete and substantially deeper.

### Cited Findings

- T20 already lists relationship-before-opening, referral kits, proof-of-work tasks, hiring signals, skill-gap queues, interview continuity, event radar, relationship CRM, route choice, outcome learning and autonomous scheduling. [T20](../../docs/specs/T20-opportunity-action-engine.md)
- Existing career trackers already span saved roles through interviews/offers, contacts and application documents. [Huntr](https://huntr.co/product/job-tracker), [Teal](https://www.tealhq.com/tools/job-tracker)
- Existing network software already advertises continuous interaction memory, reconnect signals and people search. [Mesh](https://me.sh/), [Mesh integrations](https://me.sh/integrations), [Nexus](https://me.sh/nexus)
- Recruiting-event workflows can provide specific employer representatives, booked 1:1 access, prior encounters and follow-up records. [Handshake](https://support.joinhandshake.com/hc/en-us/articles/360049934274-Handshake-s-Guide-to-Attending-a-Virtual-Fair)

### Inferences

These are product proposals, not established uplift, claims of invention, or assertions that competitors lack them.

1. **A command center that allocates attention.** Build a Today view for actions, decisions, preparation, appointments and unresolved external outcomes; ask what the system completed, what is blocked, and what only the owner can do. Allow a weekly time budget and private calendar capacity to drive sequencing. T20 already proposes next-action ranking; deepen it into a unified owner interface, dependency chain and execution ledger. Ruthless criticism: a filterable feed makes the owner integrate the workflow mentally, even if individual cards are excellent. Parent must verify current UI before stating this as observed.

2. **A persistent employer campaign, not isolated requisition packets.** A company/team dossier links multiple openings, authentic contacts, official technical/product signals, campus/event access, prior conversations, application rules and the owner's target thesis. Show a coherent path: event encounter → requested artifact → agreed introduction → eventual opening. T20 contains routes and pre-opening relationship ideas; deepen into team identity, milestones, mutually consistent outreach and explicit stale evidence. Handshake proves richer interaction channels exist; it does not prove such channels are publicly accessible to this app.

3. **A verified model of the candidate that grows.** Maintain claim-level evidence for projects, technical depth, independent performance, strengths, unsupported requirements and real weaknesses. Turn a repeated deficit into an actual practice/project plan, validate the output independently, then revise candidate facts. T20 contains career facts and skill-gap queues; the extension is an enduring readiness model plus measured transfer to unseen tasks, rather than generating flattering text. A working app cannot manufacture experience or confirm skill from a resume keyword.

4. **A proof and inbound-opportunity engine.** Preserve useful artifacts from existing work: demo, concise technical note, measured benchmark, design explanation, contribution attribution and verified outcome. Build role-family proof collections and a user-selected shareable packet; track which artifact was actually shared and the response. T20 anticipates reuse and 60–90-minute tasks; extend to durable portfolio evolution and an optional inbound-facing proof surface. This is a proposal for the owner's competitive evidence, not a mandate for public commercialization. No auto-publication or authorship assumptions.

5. **Relationship continuity with reciprocity.** Record who asked for what, promises made, what was actually useful to the person, follow-through, last authentic interaction and current relationship uncertainty. Refresh employer/role evidence and suggest relevant helpful contributions, not only referral extraction. T20 includes CRM, intros and follow-through; the deeper product tracks obligations and mutual value across seasons. Mesh indicates the maturity bar for contact memory. Messages, social profile guesses and recurring contact counts are not proof of a strong relationship.

6. **Evidence-based employer/role due diligence.** Move beyond tier/prestige and fit score toward what the internship would actually deliver: documented team scope, supervision/mentorship evidence, project ownership, technology, location burden, compensation provenance, return-offer information if available and process expectations. Keep source age and unknowns visible. Separate "I can get this" from "this advances my desired trajectory." T20 mentions explicit company preferences and fit; a decision dossier and scenario comparison are deeper proposals. Do not infer mentorship or return-offer odds from company reputation or invented private data.

7. **Carry the mission through offer and the next cycle.** A decision workflow can preserve actual offer terms and expiry, compare competing commitments, prepare factual questions, schedule decision work and keep accepted obligations visible; later capture internship work into reviewed career evidence and the next search. T20 is strongest through networking, submission and interview prep; this extends the explicit operating loop to selecting, fulfilling and learning from the opportunity. Teal/Huntr already recognize negotiating/offer stages, so a new stage dropdown alone is parity. Autonomous negotiation or acceptance is a material commitment and needs a separate configured boundary; sophisticated offer modeling is financial guidance requiring current authoritative research.

### Gaps

- These proposals need current-app inspection to distinguish wholly missing features from partial capabilities.
- Local T20 cannot establish what will be difficult for this specific owner's data quality, accounts, school access or candidate evidence.
- More tools or actors cannot establish that this owner receives more useful opportunities. The architecture should record outputs and external outcomes without pretending sparse observations support calibrated probabilities.

## What constraints matter without shrinking the vision?

### Takeaway

Keep the ambition broad and the truth model strict. Build connectors and execution depth explicitly; never advertise access or certainty that underlying sources cannot supply.

### Cited Findings

- T20 distinguishes genuine requisition ownership, company relevance and deliverability, keeps missing eligibility unknown, revalidates action inputs, and reconciles uncertain sends/submissions. It also notes employer-authenticated application APIs and the small production VM's browser/model limitations. [T20](../../docs/specs/T20-opportunity-action-engine.md)
- Simplify documents an extension with explicit supported portals; broad compatibility is an engineering surface, not a single public apply endpoint. [Simplify](https://simplify.jobs/copilot)
- Handshake's fair participation requires logged-in platform use, school fair availability, session registration and potentially profile visibility changes. Public event discovery does not automatically grant session access or attendee identity. [Handshake](https://support.joinhandshake.com/hc/en-us/articles/360049934274-Handshake-s-Guide-to-Attending-a-Virtual-Fair)
- Mesh describes connector-specific capture behavior rather than a single universal network stream. [Mesh integrations](https://me.sh/integrations)

### Inferences

- Make evidence freshness, identity confidence and unknowns first-class product features. A richer graph increases the cost of false joins and stale employment; graph structure alone is not trustworthy intelligence.
- A useful system needs a deterministic action layer with durable records, uncertainty reconciliation and an owner-readable account of what happened. More powerful planning magnifies the need for this; it is part of product quality, not bureaucratic overhead.
- Scope connectors by supported action and data contract. Network-wide sync, event booking, arbitrary ATS execution and actual skill validation are separate engineering capabilities. None should be silently represented as completed because a model generated a plan.
- Initial value can come from structured manual import and user-provided evidence while broader lawful, authorized connectors mature. This is implementation sequencing, not a recommendation to stop building.

### Gaps

- No mailbox/calendar/social account was accessed, no contact messages sent, and no paid lookup activated.
- No precise cost or implementation-time commitments were derived in this pass.
- Official product pages do not resolve data-access permissions for building an integration into those products. Provider documentation and account-specific setup must be checked during implementation.
