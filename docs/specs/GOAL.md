# Drop Radar product goal

Owner-adopted goal, October 4, 2026. Status: active vision; implementation is incomplete.

Build a personal opportunity engine that understands the owner's ambition,
develops demonstrated competitive capabilities, builds credible routes into target
teams, executes permitted work, and carries the process through applications,
relationships, preparation, interviews, offers and future recruiting cycles.

The owner explicitly wants a bigger vision and continued feature development.
The earlier review's cut/freeze recommendations are not the adopted direction.
Build capabilities that share evidence, coordinate decisions and carry work through
to real outcomes, so the owner does not have to integrate disconnected automations.

## Capabilities to develop

- A Today command center coordinating time, obligations, actions and exceptions.
- Persistent company/team campaigns across people, events and requisitions.
- A candidate model linking requirements to demonstrated readiness and evidence.
- Coordinated application, referral, outreach and deadline routes.
- An evidence studio for defensible project artifacts and reusable career proof.
- Relationship continuity, reciprocity, consent and commitment tracking.
- Visible autonomous operations with receipts, correction, pause and recovery.
- Outcome learning connected to materials, routes and verified skill improvement.
- Offer decisions and continuity into internships and subsequent recruiting cycles.

T20 already proposes many ingredients; the adopted extension is their depth and
coordination. Personal internship use remains primary; optional private friend access
is secondary. Preserve truthful claims, permanent IDs, private data and explicit
account/policy authorization. Adopting this goal does not activate external actions,
authorize spending, impose a deadline or mean these capabilities are shipped.
