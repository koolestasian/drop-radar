# Drop Radar autonomous networking

**Build Drop Radar into an agent that executes the administrative work around getting hired, so your attention goes mainly to useful conversations, events and genuine assessments.** It should discover a promising job or person, choose a credible route, initiate appropriate outreach, understand replies, negotiate a mutually agreed time, put the meeting on your calendar and prepare you. After the encounter, it should complete agreed follow-through, pursue permitted introductions and keep the application moving. You configure its operating policy once; routine actions inside that policy proceed without another approval click. This expands T20's earlier draft-and-approve scope, while preserving review mode as an option. The networking literature supports parts of this design, not an autonomous-agent interview multiplier. The proposal below is ambitious product direction, researched October 4, 2026; nothing has been connected, sent, submitted or deployed.

## The product should manage relationships and routes alongside jobs

A promising opening is only one trigger. A former collaborator moving to a target team, a recruiter offering a conversation, a relevant public event, an introduction offered during a chat and an explicit assessment deadline can all create more useful work than another fresh listing. Drop Radar should maintain those activities alongside its existing job discovery. Its daily output becomes **confirmed commitments with preparation ready, progressing applications and a short exception queue**. Generated drafts are intermediate work products, not the completion metric.

For you should rank **people, routes and actions as well as jobs**. A contact who has seen your work can substantiate it; a company employee found through a public directory supplies weaker evidence. A confirmed recruiter request has a different urgency from speculative outreach. Prioritize demonstrated fit, genuine relationship context, a person's relevance to the actual team, explicit interest, deadlines and the total time a route consumes. Show reasons and unknowns rather than a fabricated interview probability. A useful recommendation could be: “Your former project partner works near this team; this role fits two verified projects; the app has requested a conversation.” Another could be: “This event has a relevant speaker, but no meeting is agreed yet; attendance is optional.”

**Referrals can carry information beyond a résumé.** Three field experiments in an online labor market found that referrals conveyed information about performance and persistence beyond observed characteristics; workers also performed particularly well with their referrers. That setting does not supply a CS internship conversion rate, but it supports favoring a person who can explain your work over an impressive title alone. ([Referral experiments](https://pallais.scholars.harvard.edu/sites/g/files/omnuum5926/files/pallais/files/referrals.pdf))

The app should resolve one active route instead of contacting everyone at an employer. It can pursue a direct application while a relationship develops when timing requires it, but suppress contradictory or duplicate approaches. Missing contact information must not block application preparation. Cold professional outreach remains a route to test; it should not receive invented relationship strength. Existing competitors already advertise application automation, contact lookup, tailoring and inbox tools. This proposed integration is not claimed as an invention or a proven market gap.

## Eight workflows should finish work instead of assigning it back

These are proposed product experiments. The executed outcomes require an enabled account and policy; their hiring return remains to be measured.

| Trigger | AI actions and executed outcome | Human exception |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A real prior contact becomes relevant** | Match a new role/company/topic to evidenced prior collaboration; inspect actual history; send an honest reconnection request; interpret the reply; negotiate time; deliver an agreed coffee chat with a brief. | Confirm an unknown relationship once; attend the conversation. A refusal stops the sequence. |
| **A target job has a credible introduction route** | Find the best substantiated connector; assemble role ID, relevant work and a forwardable explanation; request permission; complete the introduction and scheduling when offered. | Unclear permission, identity or endorsement. The app cannot speak as the intermediary. |
| **A company exposes a relevant hiring or technical signal** | Monitor permitted official career/team/event sources; connect the signal to existing interests or work; initiate a specific allowed conversation; preserve actual replies for a later opening. | Unsupported hiring inference or a substantive question the candidate cannot honestly claim to care about. |
| **A contact gives a positive reply or scheduling link** | Extract constraints, check calendars, offer feasible slots, clarify time ambiguity with the counterpart, process counterproposals, verify agreement, create one invitation/link, handle changes and prepare the agenda. | Unavailable calendar, unresolved owner preference, conflicting commitment or action outside the policy. |
| **A relevant event becomes available** | Verify access and relevance, complete supported registration, distinguish waitlist from confirmation, coordinate short meetings with reachable relevant people, reserve time and supply an itinerary. | Payment, unsupported registration, attestations or out-of-bounds travel. Owner attends. |
| **A chat or email contains a commitment** | Extract what was actually requested/offered; send the approved résumé or artifact, give a specific thanks, finish a permitted introduction and retain a factual next action. | Missing encounter evidence or a promise requiring new personal work. A short voice note can supply the missing facts. |
| **Someone explicitly invites help relevant to existing work** | Find a useful verified resource, reproduction or prior artifact; check claims; provide it under policy; track whether it solved the expressed problem and reconnect only for a real reason. | Untested code, confidential material, invented contribution or work requiring the owner's expertise. |
| **An application or interview opportunity becomes actionable** | Prepare truthful materials, execute a supported form under policy, reconcile submission evidence, capture replies/deadlines, coordinate interviews and assemble focused preparation from the actual submission. | New unsupported answers, account verification, unexpected commitments or unsupported forms. Owner performs the assessment/interview. |

The event workflow should produce **a reason to attend and, where achievable, actual agreed encounters**. A discovered listing alone cannot justify filling the calendar. UW's event infrastructure supports campus/departmental publication and registration features, but this does not establish a universal registration API or complete coverage. Start with individually verified feeds/pages and supported forms, then expand to other campus, alumni, company and professional events. Public speakers are context, not a private attendee list or permission for indiscriminate outreach. ([UW event infrastructure](https://it.uw.edu/summary/uw-event-calendar/))

The help workflow is deliberately demanding. An existing demo that resolves an expressed issue can be useful. Automatically generating irrelevant “value” documents to manufacture a favor is easy to automate and hard to justify. New speculative projects should not become compulsory work that consumes the time this app saved. Similarly, after a coffee chat the app can complete logistics, but it cannot know what was promised without accessible correspondence, a consented record or a minimal owner update.

## Psychology should improve relevance and responsiveness

Dormant relationships are an attractive starting point because they contain actual history. A study of Executive MBA students reconnecting with former contacts found useful knowledge with novelty and retained trust. It was not a randomized job-search trial, and locating contacts and catching up added unmeasured costs. The product implication is to surface and activate real former relationships, not label every LinkedIn connection a warm tie. ([Dormant-tie study](https://business.gwu.edu/sites/g/files/zaxdzs5326/files/15_FP.SP_Walter.J_15levin_2011a.pdf))

Experiments also found that people underestimated recipients' appreciation of positive check-ins. They measured appreciation, not interviews or the effects of unattended AI intimacy. A truthful reason to reconnect is worth testing; invented memories, concern and familiarity undermine the premise. ([Reaching-out experiments](https://www.apa.org/pubs/journals/releases/psp-pspi0000402.pdf))

Advice requests need a real question and a relevant expert. Research reported improved perceived competence when asking an expert about a difficult task, with different results for easy tasks or nonexperts. However, coauthor provenance statements report missing reproducible data for much of that paper, so this deserves lower evidentiary weight. Test a substantive, transparent question against a direct job-related request; do not automate “pick your brain” as a disguised referral funnel. ([Advice-seeking paper](https://www.hbs.edu/ris/Publication%20Files/Advice%20Seeking_59ad2c42-54d6-4b32-8517-a99eeae0a45c.pdf), [provenance qualification](https://manycoauthors.org/gino/68))

Conversation research found benefits from questions responsive to what the other person said. Its liking and speed-dating outcomes do not establish professional hiring effects. A 2025 correction reported minor errors and an independent audit with unchanged hypothesis-test conclusions. The useful inference is that **a reply should change the app's next action**: deliver the requested link, acknowledge the actual constraint or prepare a relevant follow-up question. An unchanged five-email sequence cannot do that. ([Question-asking research](https://dash.harvard.edu/entities/publication/73120379-2eb6-6bd4-e053-0100007fdf3b), [correction](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40111841/))

This is where aggressive execution needs better judgment. More senior contacts can be less relevant than former collaborators. More events can produce fewer useful conversations per hour. More personalized mail can become intrusive or wrong. The proposed system should reduce recipient effort, make its purpose clear and respond accurately; it should avoid coercive reciprocity, false urgency and fabricated endorsement. These are design judgments, not scientifically optimized message rules.

## Configure autonomy once and enforce it outside the model

The owner policy names permitted contact groups and companies, sender identity, allowed purposes, factual disclosures, attachment versions, contact cooldowns, follow-up bounds, scheduling windows, duration, travel buffers and spending limits. Routine initial messages, availability negotiation, requested document delivery, agreed invitations and supported submissions can execute inside it. Review mode remains available. New commitments, unresolved identity, unsupported facts, payments and requests outside those bounds become exceptions. Account access and policy activation require concrete setup; this research activates neither.

Counterpart ambiguity normally goes back to the counterpart automatically: “Did you mean Tuesday at 10 a.m. Pacific?” The owner need not resolve another person's vague message. Escalation is reserved for a missing owner preference, conflicting commitment or ambiguity that the allowed conversation cannot resolve.

One planning/interpretation worker is enough. Use the existing Python/FastAPI/SQLite application for private facts, contacts, conversation evidence, policy, durable workflow state, due actions and provider cursors. Let AI research relevance, compose text and extract reply facts with source spans. Let deterministic handlers resolve recipients, check policy and factual versions, validate dates/timezones, check conflicts and execute writes. A web page or email cannot expand permissions or instruct the app to reveal unrelated data. Keep heavy models, rendering and browser work outside ingestion and off the small VM; feed requests should never initiate model calls.

The lifecycle is **trigger → evidenced route → policy check → outreach → reply → negotiation → agreement → invitation → preparation → encounter → follow-through → outcome**. Declines stop the route. Rescheduling changes the existing meeting. A write timeout becomes an unknown outcome requiring reconciliation before retry, not an excuse to send again. Preserve exact content, recipient, artifact version and provider IDs so the owner can inspect what happened.

Gmail supports retrieval of conversation threads and requires the correct thread ID, reply headers and subject for threading. Its push system uses history cursors and can miss notifications, so reconciliation remains necessary; a small personal pilot can start with bounded thread polling. Reading replies also requires broader access than send-only OAuth. These are actual integration requirements, not reasons to make every message a manual task. ([Gmail threads](https://developers.google.com/workspace/gmail/api/guides/threads), [mailbox synchronization](https://developers.google.com/workspace/gmail/api/guides/push), [OAuth scopes](https://developers.google.com/workspace/gmail/api/auth/scopes))

Calendar free/busy can check authorized calendars, but another person's email does not grant their calendar access. Negotiate available times when their availability is unknown. Recheck the owner's conflicts before committing. A private hold has no attendees and expires. A meeting needs exact agreement evidence and a valid venue/link; distinguish email agreement, invitation sent and calendar acceptance. Google's event status defaults to confirmed even while attendees need to respond, so that field alone cannot establish consent. ([Free/busy](https://developers.google.com/workspace/calendar/api/v3/reference/freebusy/query), [event creation](https://developers.google.com/workspace/calendar/api/v3/reference/events/insert))

Application execution needs similarly explicit evidence. Public Greenhouse and Lever listings do not authorize submission through their employer-key APIs. Start with a supported candidate-side form worker, versioned answers and a verified attachment. Unexpected controls, login verification and uncertain outcomes produce specific exceptions; an Apply click never proves submission. ([Greenhouse API](https://docs.greenhouse.io/job-board.html), [Lever API](https://github.com/lever/postings-api#apply-to-a-job-posting))

## Prove one executing route, then expand the ambition

The first walking skeleton should take a supplied verified contact through permitted outreach, a real reply, negotiated agreement, one calendar meeting and a ready brief. This is staging for the full vision, not a replacement with another draft generator. First-week scope is approximately two to three engineering days for account/policy/data/state foundations and three to four for threaded messages, reply evidence and scheduling. With inputs and OAuth ready, that is a narrow prototype, not the whole platform.

A dependable pilot adds approximately three to five days for simulated failures, real authorized shadow evaluation, uncertain writes, concurrent replies, calendar edits and reschedule/cancellation. **Eight to twelve focused engineering days** is the proposed narrow-pilot estimate, with provider setup delays separate. These are engineering assumptions, not measured durations. Company triggers, event registrations, introduction chains, supported applications and multiple providers then expand in source-specific iterations. The prior packet/fact/answer foundations still matter; they now serve execution rather than defining the final experience.

Measure coordination minutes per genuinely agreed and held meeting, informative human replies, completed introductions/referrals, confirmed submissions, deadlines met, human interviews and total candidate hours, including attendance. Also record factual corrections, unwanted contact, duplicate actions, conflicts and model/tool cost. Compare similar relationship types and target contexts; warm collaborators versus random cold addresses cannot isolate a message effect. Sparse outcomes stay descriptive. This makes it possible to remove automations that create activity without advancing the search.

The operational interface should feel quiet: “Coffee chat agreed for Tuesday; here's the brief,” “Registration confirmed; one conversation arranged,” and “This new question needs your answer once.” Genuine discussions, learning and evaluated work remain yours. The app should take responsibility for coordination and administration around them.

**Illustrative morning output, not achieved:** a former collaborator has agreed to a Thursday coffee chat and the app handled the time change; a relevant event registration is confirmed with a short meeting arranged afterward; a supported application is submitted with confirmation evidence; an interview invitation has a prepared brief and practice scheduled. The only exception is a compensation commitment outside your policy. This is the experience to build toward, rather than waking up to ten drafts requiring ten clicks.
